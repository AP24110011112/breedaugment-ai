DATASET: Oxford-IIIT Pet Dataset

Images were downloaded from the Zenodo record:
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.8067751
Record title: The Oxford-IIIT Pet Dataset
The archive is a mirror of the dataset described on the University of Oxford Visual Geometry Group page:
https://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~vgg/data/pets/

The Oxford page states that the dataset is available under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0). The copyright of the photographs remains with their original owners. Please retain this attribution and review the linked license before redistributing this dataset or derivative materials.
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Dataset paper:
O. M. Parkhi, A. Vedaldi, A. Zisserman, and C. V. Jawahar, “Cats and Dogs,” IEEE Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR), 2012.

This project downloaded the archive named “The Oxford-IIIT Pet.zip” (MD5: 7f61d2a52733bce84d505617dd7e780a) from the Zenodo record. Train and validation folders from that archive were combined by breed in this directory; the project then creates its own stratified train/validation/test split before augmentation.
